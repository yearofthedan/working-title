# 7. Chapters separate from the tree

Status: Accepted, 2026-10-04, chapter view read-only 2026-10-07

## Context

Writers plan in story order but publish in chapter order, and regroup chapters late. Options: chapters as a level of the tree, or chapters as a separate ordering over scenes.

## Decision

A chapter is an ordered grouping of scenes in manuscript order, separate from the tree. Prose is stored on each scene. A new scene starts in a chapter of its own. A chapter's view shows its scenes' prose as one continuous, read-only text with scene breaks; the writer steps into a scene to change it.

## Consequences

Regrouping chapters never moves a scene in the plan. Editing inside the chapter view may come later if stepping in proves awkward.
