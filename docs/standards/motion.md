# Motion

**Audience:** anyone building something that moves on screen.
**Purpose:** when the app may move something, how long it may take, and when nothing moves.

- **Motion shows a change.** The app moves something only to carry the eye from where it was to where it is now, such as the graph panning to a piece, or to mark a change of state, such as a field taking focus. Decorative motion, such as a pulse, a bounce or parallax, is not used. Backed by [WCAG 2.2, 2.3.3 Animation from Interactions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html) ("eliminate unnecessary motion effects").
- **Motion is as short as the change allows, and never holds the writer up.** Small feedback, such as a field taking focus, takes about 100 ms; a card arriving or the graph panning takes 200–300 ms; nothing takes longer than 400 ms. Motion that brings something in eases out, slowing as it comes to rest. The writer can type or act while motion runs. Backed by [NN/g on animation duration](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/animation-duration/): about 100 ms for simple feedback, 200–300 ms for substantial screen changes, 400 ms as very slow, for big movements only, and ease-out for things coming to rest.
- **Nothing moves when the writer's system asks for reduced motion.** Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, every change happens in one step, panning included. This goes beyond the AA baseline, since 2.3.3 is Level AAA. Backed by [WCAG 2.3.3](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html), [MDN: prefers-reduced-motion](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/@media/prefers-reduced-motion), and its Baseline status, widely available since 2022-07-15 ([the probe](https://github.com/yearofthedan/working-title/issues/121#issuecomment-6400137498)), which [the styles standard](styles.md) requires.
- **Nothing flashes, and nothing moves on its own for long.** Nothing flashes more than three times in any one second ([2.3.1 Three Flashes or Below Threshold](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/three-flashes-or-below-threshold.html), Level A), and motion that starts on its own and lasts more than five seconds can be paused, stopped or hidden ([2.2.2 Pause, Stop, Hide](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pause-stop-hide.html), Level A). These are what the [AA baseline](../architecture.md#quality-targets) asks of motion.
- **Scrolling the writer does is not motion here.** [WCAG 2.3.3](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html) treats movement the user drives by scrolling as essential, so the rules above don't cover it.
