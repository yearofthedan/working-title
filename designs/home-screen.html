First the place, then Your projects

The first thing the app asks, once, is where projects are kept. After that the home screen is Your projects: everything in that folder, and a way to start another.

This follows the thread: every project always has a folder, the list is not only novels, the app makes a project's folder, and choosing the place comes before anything else.

First open: one question

The same step serves the new writer and the writer on a new machine. A folder with projects in it lists them. An empty one says so, and offers a first project.

More than one on the go

From inside a project, the first step of the path leads here. Your projects is what the folder holds: each project's title and its one-sentence summary, the last one opened first. One click opens it where it was left.

#217 rule: a novel starts with a title and a folder the writer chooses. The folder is chosen once, before the first project. A project then starts with a title, and the app makes its folder there.
#217 scenario: cancelling the choice of folder keeps the title. Nothing left to describe: no title has been typed when the folder is chosen.

#217: a project started without a title is "Untitled novel", and so is its folder. "Untitled project".
#217, last slice: starting another novel replaces the first in the browser, after asking. Each project keeps its own copy in the browser. Starting another asks nothing.
#3 Is not: a list of recent novels to reopen. Your projects is in. It lists what the folder holds.
